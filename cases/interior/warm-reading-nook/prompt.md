# Warm Reading Nook

## Use Case

适合室内设计灵感图、民宿页面、生活方式品牌图或编辑配图。

## Prompt

```text
Create a cozy interior design photograph of a small reading nook beside a tall window on a rainy afternoon. Include a low linen armchair, a walnut side table, a ceramic mug, stacked books, a woven rug, and a slim brass floor lamp. The room has warm plaster walls, natural oak shelves, a few green plants, and soft reflections on the window glass. Use a wide but intimate composition, eye-level camera, natural diffused daylight mixed with warm lamp light, and realistic materials. The image should feel calm, lived-in, editorial, and suitable for a boutique home design magazine.
```

## Negative Constraints

```text
Avoid overly staged furniture, impossible room geometry, clutter, unreadable book titles, harsh shadows, plastic textures, and distorted window frames.
```

## Suggested Parameters

- Model: GPT Image 2
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Style: realistic interior photography
- Output: png or webp
