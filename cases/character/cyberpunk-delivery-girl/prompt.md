# Cyberpunk Delivery Girl

## Use Case

适合角色设定、游戏概念图、故事封面或头像方向探索。

## Prompt

```text
Create a cinematic character concept image of a young futuristic delivery rider standing under neon rain in a dense cyberpunk alley. She wears a lightweight reflective courier jacket, a compact backpack with glowing route indicators, fingerless gloves, and practical streetwear boots. Her expression is calm and focused, with rain droplets on her hair and jacket. The background shows layered signs, wet pavement reflections, distant food stalls, and soft steam. Use a three-quarter body composition, 35mm lens feeling, teal and magenta neon lighting, and a grounded realistic illustration style. The character should feel capable, modern, and memorable without looking like an existing franchise character.
```

## Negative Constraints

```text
Avoid copyrighted logos, unreadable face, exaggerated anatomy, extra fingers, oversexualized clothing, weapon focus, and chaotic background clutter.
```

## Suggested Parameters

- Model: GPT Image 2
- Aspect ratio: 2:3
- Style: cinematic character concept
- Output: png or webp
