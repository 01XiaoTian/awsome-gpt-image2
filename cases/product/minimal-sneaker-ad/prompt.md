# Minimal Sneaker Ad

## Use Case

适合生成运动鞋新品发布、电商主图或社交媒体广告图。

## Prompt

```text
Create a premium studio product photograph of a futuristic white running sneaker floating slightly above a matte stone pedestal. The sneaker has subtle silver mesh panels, translucent air cushioning, and precise stitched details. Use a clean off-white background with a soft blue-gray shadow gradient. The composition is centered, with generous negative space above the product for advertising copy. Lighting is soft but directional, like a high-end product campaign, with crisp material highlights and realistic contact shadows. The image should feel minimal, expensive, modern, and ready for an ecommerce hero banner.
```

## Negative Constraints

```text
Avoid visible brand logos, messy laces, warped soles, extra shoes, distorted text, cluttered background, low-resolution fabric texture, and unrealistic reflections.
```

## Suggested Parameters

- Model: GPT Image 2
- Aspect ratio: 4:5
- Style: realistic product photography
- Output: png or webp
